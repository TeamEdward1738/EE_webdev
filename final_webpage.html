Part 1: A Little Background of Devil's kitchen

Devil's Kitchen is known as a mini Bryce Canyon because of its similar red rock formations. The trail is fairly short and well maintained its easy to hike. The Trail runs through the forest to a platform to obsrve the beautiful red rock formations

Part Two: The Trailhead

To reach this trailhead from Payson, follow the Nebo loop road into Payson canyon and then up the mountain side. The trailhead is about 28 miles from the mouth of the canyon. It is is easy to see the sign from the road. You can also reach the trailhead by driving the Nebo loop road from the Nephi Side of the canyon Nephi is about a 20 minute drive from Payson.

Part 3: Overlook + Why You Should Go See It

From the trailhead, follow the signed trail south into the forest. The trail is easy to follow and is more of a walk than a hike. Devils Kitchen is an amazing site, with red rocks and red dirt on top of a mountain in the middle of a gorgeous forest. The entire Nebo Loop is a very beautiful secnis mountain drive, the loop is particularly beautiful in the fall with all the changing colors. If you are driving the Nebo loop , Devils Kitchen is a must-see Attraction, well wotrth the few minutes requires to "hike" to the overlook
