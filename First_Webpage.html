Part 1: The Plot of the First Movie of the Twilight Saga

The first movie of Twilight is about a seventeen year old girl named Bella Swan who moves from Phoneix, Arizona to Forks, Washington. While living in Forks she meets a guy named Edward Cullen while interacting with Edward she discovers he's not a normal human being his eyes change colors periodically and he has cold, almost translucient skin. Later Bella does some research and discovers that Edward is a vampire. Even though she found out about him being a vampire she falls in love with him and he falls in love with her. While out playing baseball with edward's family three other vampires appear named Victoria, James, and Laurent. They discover that Bella is a human. James is a tracker and decides he is going to kill bella. Bella is badly hurt when James tries to kill her but Edward manages to save her.

Part 2: The Plot of the Second Movie of the Twilight Saga

Edward decides to abrubtly leave to protect Bella from the supernatural world. When Edward leaves Bella falls into a depression,she finds comfort in her childhood friend Jacob Black. Jacob turns out to be a shape-shifting werewolf. Edward mistakenly believes bella has died and travles to Volterra, Italy,to convince the the ruling vampire concil(the Volturi) to kill him. Bella and Edward's sister Alice rush to save him just in time

Part 3: The Plot of the Third Movie of the Twilight Saga

As High School graduation aproached, Seatle is under attack by a group of mysterious murders committed by an army of newborn vampires. This army was created by Victoria for the revenge of her mate James. Bella is brought into a love triangle between Edward and Jacob. The Cullen Family And the Quileute Werewolf park decide to form a temporary alliance to destroy the newborn vampire army, Bella Accepts a marriage proposal from Edward

Part 4: The Plot of the Fourth Movie of the Twilight Saga

After they graduate from high school Bella and Edward get married. they go on there honeymoon, while on their honeymoon Bella somehow becomes pregnant. Her pregnancy is a rapid life threatening supernatural occurence. Bella's body becomes super fragile. Eventually the majority of Bella's bone break and she is rushed into an emergency surgery to get the baby out. Edward is faced with a tough decision to turn Bella into a vampire to save her life. He finds the strength to save her for his family and his newborn daughter Renesme.

Part 5: The Plot of the Fifth and Final Movie of the Twilight Saga

Bella has know become a vampire and is learning how to control her thrist and control her new abilities. While out playing with her daughter there cousin from denali sees her daughter and thinks they turned a child in to a vampire which is illegal in their community. Their cousin goes to the Volturi to report the "crime". The Volturi then come after Bella and Edward. The cullens go to many parts of the world to create a small army to help fight against the Volturi if needed. When the volturi approach Alice shows them what is going to happen to them if they attack so they retreat and everyone lives happily ever after.
